    • Interaction 2: Point straight up → Bell

    What: Raise the phone so its top edge points at the sky and a church bell strikes once.

    Why: Bells hang above us in towers, so you look and point up to them. Use case: an audio tour where raising your phone to a church tower rings its bell. Each raise is a single, separate gesture, so it gives a single strike. The bell re-arms once the phone is lowered below 60°, which stops it ringing again and again while you hold it up.

    How to use: Tap Turn On DSP, allow motion access, then lift the phone upright (Rotation X around 90). Lower it and raise it again to ring again.
