    • Interaction 3: Tilt side to side → Brass

    What: Hold the phone flat in your hand and tilt it left or right: the more you tilt, the harder the brass is blown.

    Why: Tilt works like breath pressure. Level means no breath and no sound; more tilt means a stronger note. It is a continuous mapping, so you hear the effect of your movement all the time. Use case: an accessible instrument for people who cannot blow into a real one. The 10° dead zone stops a slight wobble from making a sound.

    How to use: Tap Turn On DSP, allow motion access, hold the phone face up and roll it left and right. The “turned” line shows the current pressure.
