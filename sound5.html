    • Interaction 5: Raise above your head → Hallelujah

    What: Lift the phone above your head like the Statue of Liberty’s torch and hold it there, and a choir sings “Hallelujah!”.

    Why: Raising your arm overhead is a culturally loaded gesture of triumph (a torch, a trophy lift), so it deserves a triumphant, heavenly sound. A phone cannot measure its height, so the interaction looks for a quick upward lift followed by the phone pointing at the sky or tipped back. Use case: celebrating a goal or a finished task. The choir is synthesised in the browser, based on Handel’s Hallelujah chorus (1741).

    How to use: Tap Turn On DSP, allow motion access, then raise the phone overhead in one movement and hold it for a moment. Lower it to reset. The “turned” line shows lift / pose / ready.
