    • Interaction 1: Shake → Thunder

    What: Shake the phone hard and a thunderclap rumbles.

    Why: Shaking is a sudden, violent burst of energy, and so is thunder, so the action and the sound feel equally intense. Use case: a “shake to make a storm” moment in a children’s weather app.

    How to use: Tap Turn On DSP, allow motion access, then give the phone a firm shake. Change the shake threshold slider if it is too sensitive or not sensitive enough.
