    • Interaction 4: Shake like a maraca → Maraca

    What: Shake the phone like a maraca. Every flick rattles the beads, and harder flicks give louder, denser rattles.

    Why: The gesture is the instrument: you play the phone exactly as you would play a real maraca, so no learning is needed. A rattle fires the moment a flick starts, so the sound stays tight to your rhythm. Use case: a rhythm game or a party instrument for a group. The sound is synthesised in the browser with a shaker model (many tiny bead collisions inside a resonant gourd).

    How to use: Tap Turn On DSP, allow motion access, then shake in short, sharp flicks to a rhythm. The “shaken” line counts the rattles.
